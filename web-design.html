Questions

Good To Know

What's in the complimentary audit?

We review your socials, website and paid media, then take you through what's working, what's costing you and what we'd do first. It's yours to keep whether or not we end up working together.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come together. We agree a timeline at the start and keep you updated against it.

Will I be able to update it myself?

Yes. We set the site up so the things that change often, like menus, events, team and news, are easy for you to edit, and we're there for anything bigger.

Do you write the copy and take the photos?

Yes. Copywriting, photography and video are part of the build, which is why our sites don't rely on placeholder text and stock images.

Can you connect bookings, enquiries or payments?

Yes. We connect booking systems, enquiry forms, email platforms, gift cards and online stores as part of the build.
