Our Story

Centre House was built in Sydney in 2021 with a simple idea: bring everything a brand needs under one roof. Strategy, content, photography, video, paid media — done by people who actually care about the outcome, not just the invoice.

We've grown from the ground up working with everyone from local hospitality groups and independent labels to NRL clubs and national brands. Every client, big or small, gets the same thing — our full attention, honest thinking, and work we're genuinely proud to put our name on.

We're not a revolving door of account managers and freelancers. We're a core team that gets deep into your brand, shows up on set, and stays in the trenches with you until the job is done right.

How We Work

Our Approach

01
We Listen First

Every engagement starts with understanding your brand, your audience, and what success actually looks like for you. No templates, no assumptions.

02
We Build the Strategy

Strategy before execution, always. We map out what we're going to make, why it'll work, and how we'll measure it — before a single camera is picked up.

03
We Show Up On Set

We're hands-on. The people who planned your campaign are the same people on the shoot and in the edit. No handoffs, no lost context, no excuses.

04
We Deliver & Optimise

Launch is the beginning, not the end. We track performance, feed insights back into the creative, and keep pushing until the numbers move.

05
We Stay in the Trenches

We work on retainer with most of our clients because the best results come from long-term relationships, not one-off projects.

06
We Tell the Truth

If something isn't working, we'll say so. If we think there's a better way, we'll say that too. Honest advice, always — even when it's uncomfortable.
