Who We Are

A Team Built to Make Things Move.

Centre House is a Sydney-based creative marketing agency built for brands that want to grow. We combine strategy, content, and paid media into one cohesive offering — so everything we produce works harder and goes further.

We're a small team with a big output. Every client gets our full attention, genuine creative thinking, and a team that genuinely cares about the result.

Our Mission
To help ambitious brands cut through the noise with content that connects and campaigns that convert.
Our Approach
Strategy-first, creative-led. We don't make things that look good and do nothing — everything is built to perform.
Our Promise
Honest communication, premium work, and a team that's as invested in your success as you are.
