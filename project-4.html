Eels × Subaru

Capturing the on-field moment a lucky fan won a Subaru at Eels v Roosters.

Project 04 / 07
Client
Parramatta Eels & Subaru Australia
Services
Brand Activation
Event Coverage
Social Media
Year
2025
Industry
Sports & Brand Activation

Centre House Media captured the Parramatta Eels and Subaru's major fan activation at the 2025 Eels v Roosters match: a money-can't-buy moment where one lucky fan walked away with a brand new Subaru on game day.

Our role was to document the activation in full: the build-up, the on-field reveal, the winner's reaction, and the energy of a packed stadium watching it all unfold. We worked across video and photography to deliver a content suite that served both brands (Subaru's national marketing channels and the Eels' fan community), capturing the kind of authentic, high-emotion moment that only live sport can produce.

The brief called for fast turnaround and the ability to read the room, knowing when to push in close and when to pull back and let the moment breathe. The result is a body of work that lives well beyond match day: hero edits, social cut-downs, stills for press and partner activation, and a permanent record of one of the standout brand moments of the Eels' season.
