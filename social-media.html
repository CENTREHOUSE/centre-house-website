Questions

Good To Know

What's in the complimentary audit?

We review your socials, website and paid media, then take you through what's working, what's costing you and what we'd do first. It's yours to keep whether or not we end up working together.

Do you shoot the content yourselves?

Yes. Our photographers and videographers shoot on site, which is why the content looks like your business and not a stock library.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn are the usual mix. We'll recommend where to focus based on where your customers spend their time.

Do we approve posts before they go live?

Yes. You see the month's content and captions ahead of time and nothing is published without your sign-off.

How does organic work with paid ads?

Organic builds the audience and the proof; paid puts your best content in front of more of the right people. They work best run by the same team.
