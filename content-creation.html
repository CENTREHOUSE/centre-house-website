Questions

Good To Know

What's in the complimentary audit?

We review your socials, website and paid media, then take you through what's working, what's costing you and what we'd do first. It's yours to keep whether or not we end up working together.

Do you shoot photo and video on the same day?

Yes. Our crew covers both, so one shoot day delivers stills, hero video and short-form cut-downs.

Can you handle concepts and creative direction?

Yes. We write the concepts, plan the shoot and direct on the day, or work to your brief if you already have one.

How fast is turnaround?

Social edits are usually back within days, campaign films within a couple of weeks. We agree timings before the shoot.

Do you travel?

We're based in Sydney and regularly shoot across Australia and overseas.
