Earls Collection

Shooting the seasons for Earls Collection's 2025 campaign run.

Project 01 / 08
Client
Earls Collection
Services

Photography
Videography
Year

Centre House Media brought two of Earls Collection's standout 2025 campaigns to life: the Autumn Knit campaign and the Winter “Two Worlds” campaign, producing the full creative capture across video and photography.

The Autumn Knit campaign leaned into texture, warmth and quiet sophistication, the kind of considered styling and tonal palette that lets the product speak. For Winter's “Two Worlds,” we shifted gear: a more conceptual, narrative-led shoot exploring contrast and duality, with a visual language built to feel cinematic and editorial in equal measure.

Across both campaigns we delivered a complete suite of assets (hero film, social cut-downs, lookbook photography and e-commerce imagery), each tuned to the season's mood while holding the threads of the Earls Collection brand world. The result is creative that doesn't just sell the collection, but builds the universe around it.
