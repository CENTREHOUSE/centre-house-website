Saint Peter

Capturing the kitchen at one of Australia's best restaurants, alongside special guests Rasmus Munk, Peter Doyle and Virgilio Martínez.

Centre House Media has captured the creative life of Saint Peter, one of Australia's most celebrated restaurants and the home of chef Josh Niland, across an ongoing body of work documenting the energy, craft and culinary collaboration that defines the kitchen.

Our role has been to translate the intensity of a working fine-dining restaurant into content that holds its weight on screen: the pace of service, the precision of plating, the quiet focus before doors open. Beyond the day-to-day, we've had the privilege of capturing Saint Peter's collaborations with some of the world's most influential chefs (Rasmus Munk of Alchemist, Copenhagen; Peter Doyle; and Virgilio Martínez of Central, Lima), bringing each guest residency to life through film and photography.

These are rare moments, global culinary minds meeting in one kitchen, and the content has to match the calibre. We work with a restrained, editorial sensibility: natural light, considered framing, and a documentary instinct that lets the food, the craft and the people lead. The result is a visual archive that sits at the level of the restaurant itself.
